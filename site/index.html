Know Thyself.

The Soul Map

A whole human understanding system. It maps how you are built, then shapes how you work, and how your AI works with you, to fit.

A 40 minute interview on your Mac that maps how you work. It writes two documents: a report for you, and a soul file your AI tools read so they work the way you do.

Human Design and Gene Keys from your birth data. Enneagram, attachment, Big Five, chronotype and values from your answers. Your Otter and Granola meetings, if you connect them.

Install on your Mac

Open Terminal, paste this line and press Return.

curl -fsSL https://soulmap.nextmissionsi.com/install.sh | bash

About 5 minutes. No admin password. It opens the Soul Map in your browser when it is done.

Who does the thinking

You pick one when you start. You can change it later.

Claude (recommended)

Claude by Anthropic reads your answers and writes your report. Uses your own Anthropic API key, about 30 cents a report.

Only this Mac

A free model runs on your Mac. Nothing you say leaves it. One download of about 5 GB.

What you get

  1. Your report: how you are built, what drives you, how you work best, where you get stuck.
  2. Your soul file: instructions for Claude, ChatGPT and other AI tools on how to work with you. One click adds it to Claude Code.
  3. Results that stay current: connected meetings sync every day, and the report updates when the picture changes.

Your data

  • Your answers, chart and reports are stored on your Mac.
  • From meetings, only your own lines are kept. Other people's words are never stored or sent.
  • Delete everything from one screen, any time.
Free and open source (MIT). Code: github.com/maximilian-dollaz/soulmap. Needs macOS.
